## Red Bits
Vanilla style redstone additions and improvements.  
Download from: [Curseforge](http://curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/red-bits) or [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/red-bits)  
Rudimentary mod wiki available here: [HELP.md](HELP.md) 

### Issues and Contributing
If you found a critical issues, have a suggestion, or want me to take a look at your PR notify in a timely manner, notify me on Discord.
(Opening issues here directly is fine but i may not see it until the next Minecraft update cycle).
Discord invite can be found on the mod's Modrinth page.

### AI Policy
This project follows strict no no-AI policy. 
No AI generated code, assets or other works may be submitted to this project.
